# Licensing & Monetization Strategy

> Finalized decisions for the Side Cart plugin's licensing model and monetization approach.

---

## Licensing Model

### Annual License Only

**Decision:** Offer annual licenses only. No lifetime licenses.

**Rationale:**

- Annual subscriptions create predictable recurring revenue that funds ongoing development and support
- Aligns incentives between customers and development (customers get continuous updates, we get sustainable revenue)
- Avoids the risk of lifetime licenses becoming a cash advance against unbounded future work
- Simplifies pricing and reduces decision fatigue for customers
- Industry standard for modern WordPress plugins with active development
